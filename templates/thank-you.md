# Thank-you templates

Send these within 24-48 hours of a call. Waiting longer loses the thread while it's still fresh for both of you.

## 1. Standard thank-you after an informational conversation

```
Hi [Name], thank you again for the time today, I know your schedule is full.
[One specific thing you learned or found useful] was especially helpful as
I think through [your situation]. I'll keep you posted on how things go, and
please don't hesitate to reach out if there's ever anything I can do on my
end.
```

## 2. Thank-you when they offered to introduce you to someone

```
Hi [Name], thank you for the conversation and for offering to connect me
with [Contact]. I really appreciate it, and I'll follow up with them
directly so you don't have to manage anything further. Hope our paths cross
again.
```

## 3. Thank-you when they said no to a referral but gave advice instead

```
Hi [Name], thank you for being straight with me and for the advice on
[specific thing they suggested]. That's useful and I'll work on
it. I appreciated the conversation either way.
```

## What makes a thank-you note land

Name one specific thing you took from the conversation. A generic "thanks for your time" reads as an obligation you're checking off. Naming the specific piece of advice or detail shows you were actually listening, and it gives the other person a reason to remember you later.
