# Referral-ask templates

The referral ask works best as the natural end of a real conversation, not a cold request on its own. Use these after you've already talked to someone, ideally more than once.

## 1. The closing question at the end of any good conversation

This is the single highest-leverage line in the whole process. Ask it every time, even when you don't have a specific role in mind yet.

```
This has been really helpful. Is there anyone else you think I should be
talking to, either at [Company] or elsewhere in [industry/function]?
```

## 2. Asking for advice instead of asking for the referral directly

Use this when there's a specific open role and you'd rather not put the person on the spot with a direct ask.

```
I saw [Company] has an opening for [role] and I've gone ahead and applied.
Given everything you've shared with me, do you have any advice on what
would help me stand out to the hiring manager?
```

Often the person offers to flag your application or make an introduction unprompted once they've given advice. If they don't, you've still gotten something useful and kept the relationship intact.

## 3. A direct referral ask, once the relationship is established

Only use this with someone you've already had at least one real conversation with.

```
I wanted to ask you directly. Would you be comfortable referring me for the
[role] opening at [Company]? I know referring someone reflects on you, so
no pressure at all if it doesn't feel right, happy to just apply on my own
either way.
```

## 4. If they say no, or the company has no formal referral process

```
Totally understand, and thanks for being straight with me. If it's easier,
would you have any advice on how I could strengthen my application, or
know if there's a better way in?
```

## What makes this land without feeling awkward

Ask for directions, not a ride. You are asking someone to point you somewhere, not to personally carry you there and take on the risk of vouching for a stranger. Make the size of the ask match the size of the relationship. A closing "who else should I talk to" works after one conversation. A direct referral ask works better after two or three conversations, or after you've already given them something in return.
