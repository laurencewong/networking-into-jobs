# Outreach message templates

Fresh drafts, not copied from any source. Each one is a starting point. Replace every bracket, delete any line that doesn't fit, and read it out loud before you send it. If it sounds like a form letter, it is one.

The common thread across every version below is simple. Lead with something real about them, make the ask small enough to answer in one line, and say plainly that you are not asking for a job yet.

## 1. Alumni or shared background (LinkedIn connection note)

```
Hi [Name], I saw you're [current role] at [Company] and that we both went
through [shared school / program / past employer]. I'm exploring a move into
[target function/industry] and would value your perspective sometime, no
pressure at all. Would you be open to connecting?
```

## 2. Something specific they posted or wrote

```
Hi [Name], I read your [post/article/talk] on [specific topic] and the point
about [specific detail] stuck with me. I'm working through a similar question
as I look at roles in [target area], and I'd value your take if
you're ever open to a short conversation. Either way, thanks for writing it.
```

## 3. Mutual connection

```
Hi [Name], [Mutual contact] suggested I reach out. I'm looking at [target
role/function] roles and [Mutual contact] mentioned you'd have a useful
perspective given your work at [Company]. Would you have 15-20 minutes in the
next couple of weeks for a quick call?
```

## 4. Cold, no connection at all (binary-question version)

```
Hi [Name], I'm looking into [target role] at companies like [Company], and
your background in [specific area] caught my eye. One quick question, if
you have a moment. Would you say [specific role] leans more toward [option A]
or [option B] day to day? No need for more than a line, just curious how
you'd call it.
```

## 5. Reaching a recruiter first (when you have no other way in)

```
Hi [Name], I noticed you recruit for [team/function] at [Company] and that
we're both [shared detail: based in city, same alma mater, etc.]. I'm
exploring [target role] opportunities there and would love to connect.
```

Follow-up after they accept:
```
Thanks for connecting, [Name]. I've been looking closely at the [specific
role, link if public] posting and wanted to understand the day-to-day and
team culture before going further. Would you be able to connect me with
someone currently on the team for a short, casual chat?
```

## What to avoid

- Don't mention the job opening in the first message if you have no connection to the company at all. Earn the reply first.
- Don't write more than 4-5 sentences. Long first messages read as a pitch, not a question.
- Don't send the identical message to ten people in one sitting. Change at least the specific detail every time, or don't send it.
